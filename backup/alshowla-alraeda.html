Who We Are

Smart Solutions for Stronger Projects

Al-Showla Al-Raeda is a leading Libyan company specialized in the import and distribution of high-quality building and sanitary materials. Since its establishment in 2005, the company has built a strong market presence based on reliability, diversity of products, and strict adherence to international quality standards.

In 2021, the company entered a new strategic phase by becoming an exclusive importer and authorized agent for several leading global brands — strengthening its position as a vital link between international manufacturers and the Libyan market.
